LAB PROCESS // 100% RECYCLED

Every MNMLST shell is constructed directly from landfill waste and reclaimed metal cans. No two devices are identical.

01 // LANDFILL HDPE / PP PLASTIC

Post-consumer bottle caps (#2 and #5) are shredded into 5mm flakes and compressed inside silicone molds at 175°C to create semi-translucent, frosted marble patterns.

02 // RECLAIMED ALUMINUM FRAMES

Discarded aluminum cans are melted inside home-lab foundries and sand-cast into structural side rails, protecting internal E Ink displays.
